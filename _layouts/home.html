Computer Division pioneers research in computing. We conduct foundational research and seek high impact in broad areas in computing, including computer systems (e.g., storage, database, embedded, and network systems), architecture, computer security, and machine learning. Computer Division is home to 17 research groups in the School of Electrical Engineering at KAIST.
